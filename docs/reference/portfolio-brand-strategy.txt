Introduction
This document was created to analyze the current communication of
The Roots Corner and define a stronger creative and storytelling
direction for the brand.
The goal is not only to promote and sell the experience of The Roots
Corner, but also to create an emotional connection with the audience
through meaningful and visually engaging content.
We aim to build a more cinematic, lifestyle-driven and immersive
communication strategy that allows people to feel the atmosphere,
identity and experience of the brand through social media.
